Biography

Wen-Yu Dong received the B.S. degree in Electronic and Information Engineering from Sichuan University, Chengdu, China, in 2019, and the Ph.D. degree in Information and Communication Engineering from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), Beijing, China, in 2025. He is currently a Researcher with the Future Technology Research Center, China Telecom Research Institute, Beijing, China.

His research focuses on 6G network modeling and optimization, stochastic geometry, integrated space–air–ground–sea and low-altitude networks, and wireless communications. He proposed the fluid-spatiotemporal stochastic geometry (F-STSG) framework, which extends conventional spatial network modeling toward non-stationary systems by characterizing evolving spatial fields, transport dynamics, and information flow. He is further developing the theoretical foundations and extensions of F-STSG, with a growing series of works spanning its theory, methodology, and applications. F-STSG publication series →

He serves as a reviewer for several IEEE journals, including IEEE JSAC, JSTSP, TWC, TCOM, TVT, TMC, IoTJ, and VTM, among others.
